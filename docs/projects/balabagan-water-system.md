# Water System Planning Support Through GIS Mapping

![Water System Planning Support Through GIS Mapping](../assets/images/balabagan-water-system.png)

## Overview

This project involved the development of GIS maps and spatial datasets supporting the planning and feasibility assessment of a proposed Level III water supply system in Balabagan, Lanao del Sur, Philippines.

The work focused on integrating field-collected locations, service area mapping, infrastructure visualization, and hazard assessment into report-ready cartographic outputs used for planning, technical documentation, and stakeholder communication.

A combination of field validation, GIS analysis, and cartographic design was used to transform raw spatial information into decision-support products for infrastructure planning.

**Study Area:** Balabagan, Lanao del Sur, Philippines
**Project Type:** Water System Planning and Feasibility Support
**Role:** GIS Analyst, Cartographer, and Field Survey Support
**Status:** Completed

---

## Objectives

The primary objective of this project was to provide spatial analysis and mapping support for water system planning by:

* Mapping intake and source locations
* Identifying proposed service areas
* Visualizing infrastructure planning components
* Integrating hazard information into planning outputs
* Producing report-ready maps for technical reports and feasibility documentation

---

## Field Data Collection

Field validation activities were conducted to support the spatial database used throughout the project.

Responsibilities included:

* Geotagging intake and source locations
* Collecting survey reference points
* Recording and validating geographic coordinates
* Integrating field-collected data into GIS workflows

These field datasets served as key reference inputs for mapping, planning, and reporting activities.

---

## Methodology

### Spatial Data Preparation

Spatial datasets were collected, reviewed, and organized from multiple sources, including:

* OpenStreetMap
* UP NOAH
* Administrative boundary datasets
* Satellite imagery basemaps
* Field-collected coordinates

All datasets were standardized and prepared for analysis and cartographic production.

---

### Infrastructure and Service Area Mapping

GIS workflows were used to map and visualize:

* Intake locations
* Water source locations
* Existing settlements
* Built-up areas
* Proposed service areas
* Supporting infrastructure references

These outputs provided a spatial framework for planning and feasibility assessment.

---

### Hazard Assessment Integration

Environmental hazard datasets were incorporated to support risk-aware planning.

Hazard layers included:

* Flood Hazards
* Landslide Susceptibility
* Storm Surge Advisories

These datasets were integrated into planning maps to help identify potential environmental constraints.

---

### Cartographic Production

Formal map layouts were produced for inclusion in technical reports and stakeholder presentations.

Map layouts included:

* Coordinate grids
* Scale bars
* North arrows
* Legends
* Inset maps
* Source references
* Consistent cartographic styling

All outputs were designed to meet professional reporting requirements.
