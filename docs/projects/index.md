# Projects

A selection of my geospatial projects focused on spatial decision support, infrastructure planning, hazard assessment, watershed analysis, and remote sensing.

---

![](../assets/images/ServiceArea.png)

**[GIS Support for Municipal Water Infrastructure Planning](balabagan-water-system.md)**

A municipal infrastructure GIS project supporting the planning and feasibility assessment of a proposed Level III Water System in Balabagan, Lanao del Sur, Philippines.

The work combined service area delineation, building and road digitization, GPS-based field validation, and flood, landslide, and storm surge hazard overlays to produce publication-ready figures for technical reports and infrastructure planning.

`QGIS` `Infrastructure Planning` `Hazard Assessment` `Field Validation`

**[Automatic Weather Station Suitability Map](aws-suitability.md)**

A GIS-based site suitability analysis identifying appropriate locations for establishing Automatic Weather Stations across the Province of Bukidnon, Philippines.

Using the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP), multiple spatial criteria were weighted and combined to produce a province-wide suitability map supporting weather monitoring infrastructure placement and decision-making.

`QGIS` `Site Suitability` `AHP` `Spatial Decision Support`

**[Watershed Catchment Area Mapping for Water Resource Planning](water-system-development.md)**

A GIS-based watershed and catchment mapping project in Balabagan, Lanao del Sur, using satellite-derived elevation data and terrain analysis to support water resource planning.

The workflow involved DEM extraction, hydrological preprocessing, stream network generation, and catchment delineation to evaluate drainage patterns and potential source-area contributions.

`QGIS` `Watershed Analysis` `Copernicus DEM` `Terrain Analysis`

**[Nature-based Solution: Proposed Riparian Buffer Restoration and Protection Strategy for Pulangui River Basin, Bukidnon](pulangui.md)**

A GIS-based nature-based solution project proposing riparian buffer restoration and protection strategies for the Pulangui River Basin in Bukidnon, Philippines.

The workflow integrated terrain analysis, hydrologic modeling, land cover exposure, and riparian buffer assessment to identify sediment-prone and degraded areas requiring ecological restoration. The project was developed as an entry to the Geographic Innovations for Development Solutions, Inc. (GRIDS) Mapping Contest and was awarded a Consolation Prize.

`QGIS` `Watershed Planning` `Nature-based Solutions` `Flood Resilience`

**[Regional Vegetation Health Monitoring System](ndvi-tool.md)**

An interactive Google Earth Engine application for calculating and visualizing the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) across provinces of Northern Mindanao, Philippines.

Designed as a passion project and presented at an exhibit, the app makes satellite remote sensing more accessible through province-level NDVI visualization, date filtering, and vegetation condition summaries.

**[Unsupervised Land Cover Classification App](unsupervised-classification.md)**

A remote sensing and machine learning project applying unsupervised K-Means clustering to Landsat 8 imagery for land cover classification in Malaybalay City, Bukidnon, Philippines.

The app generates a seven-class land cover map and provides barangay-level area summaries, giving users an interactive way to explore land cover patterns and export results.

`Google Earth Engine` `Landsat 8` `K-Means` `Land Cover`
